## Honest tradeoffs surfaced

**Why the pixelmatch floor rose instead of fell (24.52% vs predicted 8–14%):**

The Phase 2 pixelmatch floor of 18.74% was measured at a 11 847 px page height. The page was compressed ~9.3× into the 1280 px reference at comparison time. At this extreme compression ratio, most content areas became dark blobs indistinguishable from the dark design reference — yielding a deceptively low diff ratio. After Phase 02.1, the page height dropped to 7 651 px (compressed ~5.97× into 1280 px). At this less-extreme compression, more distinct structural content is visible in both images, revealing more differences against the design reference's highly compressed layout. The pixelmatch comparison is not scale-invariant: a shorter page at moderate compression differs more visibly from the reference than a very tall page at extreme compression, even though the shorter page is structurally closer to the reference's intent.

**D-03b gate design assumption vs reality:** D-03b assumed the pixelmatch floor would improve monotonically as the SPA page length approached the reference's 1280 px. This assumption was correct directionally for very large compressions but breaks at the moderate compression range (5–7×) where content differences become more visible. The floor should continue improving as the page approaches the reference's density band (Option D/E cuts toward 5000–6000 px), but that work exceeds Phase 02.1's locked scope.

**CONTEXT.md band evolution:** The originally-locked 5 000–6 000 px ambition (from the initial CONTEXT analysis anchored on the screenshot-harness 12 000 px figure) was updated on 2026-05-07 to the **7 000–8 000 px pragmatic band** after `02.1-RESEARCH.md` empirically demonstrated that reaching 5 000–6 000 px requires Option D (broader within-section cuts beyond the top 3) or Option E (component-prominence cuts), both approaching D-02b risk. The measured 7 651 px lands comfortably in the updated D-01 7 000–8 000 band. The 5 000–6 000 ambition remains achievable only via Phase 8 polish passes that founder hasn't authorized.
