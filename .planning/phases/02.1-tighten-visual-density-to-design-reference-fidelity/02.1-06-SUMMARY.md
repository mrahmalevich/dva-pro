## Honest Tradeoff Narrative

The Phase 2 close-out measured pixelmatch floor was 18.74% at a fullPage height of 11 847 px. The research phase predicted the post-densification floor would fall to 8-14% as the page shrank from 11 847 → ~7 741 px. This prediction was based on the intuition that a shorter page compressed less aggressively into the 1280 px reference frame would show fewer structural differences. The prediction was wrong in the direction it assumed. The measured floor rose from 18.74% to 24.52%.

The explanation: at 11 847 px compressed to 1280 px (9.3× ratio), every section is reduced to a few pixels of dark content. The reference is also a dark, compact image. Most pixels agree on "dark", yielding a deceptively low floor. At 7 651 px compressed to 1280 px (5.97× ratio), sections are still compressed but at a more moderate ratio — structural content (headings, cards, whitespace) becomes more visible and diverges more from the reference's different structural proportions. The pixelmatch metric measures "are these two 605×1280 images similar?" not "is the page as tall as the reference?" — and the shorter page at moderate compression diverges more visibly from the reference's design structure than the very tall page at extreme compression.

The density target was achieved: 7 651 px is firmly in the D-01 7 000–8 000 band, representing a −4 196 px (−35%) harness reduction from the Phase 2 baseline. The golden threshold was re-calibrated to the actual empirical measurement. The 5 000–6 000 px band (originally locked, then relaxed to 7 000–8 000 on 2026-05-07) remains achievable only via Option D/E cuts (broader within-section reductions or component-prominence restructuring) which were explicitly out-of-scope under D-01b.
