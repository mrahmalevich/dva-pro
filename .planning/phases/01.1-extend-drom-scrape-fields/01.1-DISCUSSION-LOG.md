## Round 2 — Resume granularity

**Question:** Cursor today checkpoints brand+model. With ≈30 trims per generation at probe-down 5–10 s pace, a mid-generation crash costs ≈ 3–5 min of refetch on resume. The BMW pilot runs ≈ 5–7 h total. How granular should resume be?

**Options:**
1. Per-generation (no change to cursor) — recommended
2. Per-trim (extend cursor.json) — adds `lastComplectationIndex`
3. Per-trim with throttled writes (every 5 trims)

**User selection:** Per-trim (extend cursor.json)

**Decisions locked:** D-01..D-04 in CONTEXT.md
- Add `lastComplectationIndex: number | null` to `cursor.json`
- Cursor written every trim (~ 2 000 writes for BMW pilot — cheap)
- Backward-compat: missing index loads as `null`, loop starts from 0
- Open for planner: whether `lastGenerationSlug` also needs to enter cursor schema

## Round 3 — Coverage metric formula

**Question:** `report.json.field_coverage[group] ≥ 0.70` needs a precise formula. The six groups have different leaf counts. Three sensible formulas yield different gate behavior on the same data.

**Options:**
1. Per-leaf rate — mean across group's leaves (most forgiving)
2. Per-trim rate — ratio of fully-populated trims (strictest)
3. Per-trim rate with majority threshold (≥ 2/3 of leaves) (compromise)
4. Min per-leaf rate — weakest leaf wins

**User selection:** Per-trim rate with majority threshold (≥ 2/3 of leaves)

**Decisions locked:** D-05..D-08 in CONTEXT.md
- Formula: `count(trims where ≥ ⌈2/3 × leaf_count⌉ leaves non-null) / total_trims`
- Precomputed per-group thresholds (5/2/4/6/4/2 for identity/pricing/drivetrain/dimensions/comfort/tires)
- Pricing and Tires collapse to "all leaves required" (intentional — partial extraction non-informative for 2-leaf groups)
- `field_coverage` is a top-level object in `report.json` next to `image_failure_rate`
