## Decisions Made

See `key-decisions` in the frontmatter. The substantive ones:

1. **Strategy B locked** — typed core (now 12 + 10 + 5 leaves across drivetrain/dimensions/chassis) + open-ended features bag for ~130 long-tail rows. Prevents Phase 03 from inheriting a schema migration when DB lands.
2. **D-06 thresholds frozen at original counts** — drivetrain stays at 4 (of original 6), dimensions stays at 6 (of original 9). Sparse new leaves (turbo, payload_kg, hybrid_*) on legacy generations would otherwise demote rates artificially. Plan 04 will revisit thresholds after the BMW X5 re-scrape produces real telemetry.
3. **Chassis + features_density floors = 0 in Plan 01** — schema lands now, Plan 04 sets floors after Plan 02's parser produces real values. Avoids gating Wave 2 on speculative thresholds.
