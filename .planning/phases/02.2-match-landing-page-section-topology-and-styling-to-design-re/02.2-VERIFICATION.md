## Honest tradeoffs

**Pixelmatch ratio rose from 24.52% (02.1 baseline) to 26.18% (02.2 post-restructure).**

This is within the accepted band (threshold 0.28). The +1.66 pp increase follows the same mechanism as Phase 02.1's honest tradeoff: adding and restructuring sections changes which visual regions produce pixel-level diffs at the ~5.6× compression used by the test. Section topology fidelity is the goal of Phase 02.2; pixelmatch numeric is a regression guard, not a fidelity score. The threshold was not raised because the test already passes at 0.28.

**DIFF_THRESHOLD kept at 0.28 (not re-computed via formula).**

The D-08 formula `ceil((measured + 0.03) × 100) / 100` would yield `ceil(29.18) / 100 = 0.30`. Since the test already passes at 0.28 (providing 1.82 pp of headroom over the measured floor), no threshold change was required or desirable. Lowering headroom by bumping the threshold is unnecessary until the floor rises above the current band.
